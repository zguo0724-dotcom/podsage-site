PodSage Support

PodSage is a podcast player for English learners on iPhone and iPad: read along with every episode, see a translation under every sentence, and look up any word.

Contact

Questions, problems or ideas: write to zipguo@icloud.com. Please say which iPhone or iPad you use and what happened. Never send passwords or payment details.

PodSage Pro subscription

Manage or cancel. In PodSage, open You → Settings → PodSage Pro → Manage Subscription. You can also open the Settings app on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Apple handles payments, renewals and refunds.

Restore a purchase. On a new device or after reinstalling, open the PodSage Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that bought the subscription.

Monthly allowances. AI features have monthly allowances. Your current use is shown in Settings → PodSage Pro. Allowances start again on the first day of each month.

Common questions

An episode has no transcript yet. PodSage uses the publisher’s transcript when there is one. When there is not, it creates one on your device. For a long episode this can take several minutes; the text appears as it becomes ready.

The translation does not appear. Translations are made on your device with Apple’s Translation. The first time you use a language, your device may need to download it. Connect to the internet and try again.

AI features do not answer. AI explanations, translation checks, chapters and mind maps need PodSage Pro, an internet connection, and “Allow AI features” turned on in Settings.
