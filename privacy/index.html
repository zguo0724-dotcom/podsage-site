Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

PodSage helps you listen to podcasts and learn English. The App Store app has no PodSage accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no ads added by PodSage. Podcast publishers may include ads in their own audio.

What stays on your device

Your followed shows, listening position, downloaded audio, transcripts, translations, saved words, learning cards, clips, review progress and settings are stored on your device. PodSage does not upload your library or listening history to its own server. Your device’s normal Apple backup settings may include app data; downloaded audio and speech recognition assets are excluded from backup.

Apple’s Translation and speech recognition run on your device. PodSage does not upload audio for transcription. Apple may download language and speech assets when needed. If needed, an additional on-device recognizer downloads from Hugging Face; that download does not include your audio or transcripts.

Finding and playing podcasts

Search words go to Apple’s iTunes Search service. Apple also supplies podcast listings and charts. Podcast feeds, artwork, published transcripts, chapters and audio come directly from publishers or their hosting services. Those services receive the requests needed to deliver the content, including your IP address, and may apply their own privacy policies. PodSage does not control those services or the ads inside a podcast.

Optional PodSage Pro AI features

Pro AI features send the words and sentences you look up, nearby transcript sentences, sentences and translations being checked, show and episode titles, and your translation language. Requests can also include your selected learning level and saved phrase context. AI chapters and mind maps use the episode’s transcript; changing a learning card’s language sends that card. Audio is not sent to the AI server.

These requests go through PodSage’s server hosted by Supabase in the United States, then to Anthropic’s Claude service to produce the answer. Your signed App Store subscription proof goes to PodSage’s server to check access; it is not included in the AI prompt sent to Anthropic. Only the text needed for the requested feature is sent, rather than your whole library.

PodSage does not save request text, transcripts, learning cards or AI answers on its server after processing. Answers may be saved on your device. The server retains monthly request counts, token totals, costs and rate-limit records linked to your original App Store subscription ID. Technical logs use a short hash of that ID and include request type, model, timing, token counts, costs and error information. They do not deliberately log request text, answers or signed subscription proofs. There is currently no automatic deletion schedule for monthly usage records.

Your AI inputs and answers are not used to train AI models under Anthropic’s standard commercial API terms. Anthropic normally deletes API inputs and answers within 30 days, with exceptions for safety or legal requirements. See Anthropic’s API data retention and model training policy.

You can use PodSage without Pro. AI sharing happens when you use Pro AI features, including enabled translation checking while listening. If you cancel, new AI requests stop when your subscription or trial access ends. Cancellation does not erase existing usage records. Free on-device translation and transcription remain available.

Purchases

Apple handles subscription payments, trials, renewals, refunds and restoration through the App Store. PodSage receives subscription status and signed transaction information to unlock Pro and enforce its allowances. PodSage does not receive your payment card details or your Apple Account password. You can manage or cancel Pro in Settings → PodSage Pro → Manage Subscription, or in Apple’s subscription settings.

Your choices and deletion

You can delete downloads in Settings, and remove saved items in the app. Deleting the app removes its local data; Apple backups are managed separately in your device settings. Deleting the app does not cancel an Apple subscription or delete server usage records. For privacy questions or a request to delete subscription usage records, contact the developer through the App Support link on PodSage’s App Store page. A subscription reference may be needed to locate those records; never send payment details or passwords.

The owner’s personal app

The separate personal edition, Podsage Pro, can send text directly to Anthropic using the owner’s own credentials. It can optionally sign in to the owner’s Vesper service and send saved learning cards and review data there. These personal integrations are absent from the App Store app described above.

Changes

This policy will be updated when PodSage’s data practices change. The current policy is available in Settings → About → Privacy Policy, including when you are offline.
